Hello! I'm Tristen Campbell, a student-athlete at Grossmont High School. Playing football has been a defining part of my high school experience, teaching me the value of relentless dedication, discipline, and trusting my teammates through every challenge on and off the field.

When I'm not on the gridiron or in the classroom, I'm a big fan of movies and shows, appreciating great visual storytelling and cinematography. I have an energetic curiosity for trying out new things—whether that's diving into hands-on creative projects, exploring technology, or picking up new hobbies that push me out of my comfort zone.

Above all, I'm focused on living in the moment, making lasting memories with friends and family, and truly enjoying life. This website brings together my high school journey, creative media, and future aspirations—thank you for stopping by!
