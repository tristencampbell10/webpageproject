The Traveler’s Mindset

Traveling has taught me that the best lessons happen outside our daily comfort zone. Whether driving down open desert highways, hiking up steep mountain switchbacks, or exploring vibrant coastal towns, every trip offers a fresh outlook on the world.

Being based in San Diego provides the ultimate launchpad—from Pacific ocean sunsets right in our backyard to snow-covered peaks and canyon landscapes just a few hours away. Here is a curated log of my favorite destinations and upcoming travel goals.

Featured Destinations & Expeditions

A collection of meaningful journeys, scenic spots, and memorable road trips:

📍 San Diego Coast, CA

Hometown Coastlines & Pacific Trails

Endless coastline adventures right at home—from the sandstone bluffs and ocean views of Torrey Pines State Reserve to sunset walks along Sunset Cliffs and Mission Beach.

#PacificOcean #TorreyPines #CoastalTrails #HomeBase
📍 Big Sur & Hwy 1, CA

Pacific Coast Highway Road Trip

One of the most iconic road trips on earth. Cruising the winding coastal highway with cliffside drops, misty mornings, ocean spray, and towering ancient coastal redwoods.

#Highway1 #BixbyBridge #BigSur #RoadTrip
📍 Yosemite National Park, CA

Granite Valleys & Half Dome

Standing dwarfed by El Capitan and Half Dome, feeling the cool mist of cascading alpine waterfalls, and experiencing starry night skies completely removed from city lights.

#Yosemite #HalfDome #SierraNevada #Alpine
📍 Sedona & Grand Canyon, AZ

Red Rock Canyons & Desert Rims

Watching the morning sunrise illuminate the layers of the Grand Canyon, followed by scrambling across vibrant red sandstone rocks and scenic vortex trails in Sedona.

#GrandCanyon #SedonaRedRocks #Southwest #DesertHorizon
📍 Big Bear & San Bernardino, CA

Alpine Escapes & Mountain Snow

A favorite winter and summer weekend getaway. Snow trips during football offseason, lakeside mountain biking, and crisp alpine air nestled in the Southern California pines.

#BigBear #MountainAir #WinterSnow #Hiking
📍 Lake Tahoe, CA / NV

Glacial Blue Waters & Emerald Bay

Crystal-clear turquoise mountain waters surrounded by pine-covered peaks. Kayaking through Emerald Bay and boulder hopping along hidden coves during warm summer afternoons.

#LakeTahoe #EmeraldBay #Kayaking #SierraLake

Future Travel Bucket List

Dream destinations and global adventures at the top of my roadmap:

International • Asia

Tokyo & Kyoto, Japan

Target: Summer 2027 • Cultural & Tech Journey

Exploring Akihabara tech centers, ancient Shinto shrines, riding the Shinkansen bullet train at 200 mph, and experiencing authentic Japanese cuisine.

Domestic • Pacific Islands

Oahu & Maui, Hawaii

Target: Winter Break • Island Adventure

Watching legendary North Shore surf swells at Banzai Pipeline, hiking the Lanikai Pillboxes, and snorkeling coral reefs in Hanauma Bay.

International • North America

Banff & Lake Louise, Canada

Target: College Expedition • Canadian Rockies

Seeing the famous glacial-fed turquoise waters in person, canoeing against rugged mountain backdrop walls, and exploring the Icefields Parkway.

International • Europe

Rome & Amalfi Coast, Italy

Target: Post-High School • Mediterranean Culture

Walking through thousand-year-old Roman history at the Colosseum, followed by dramatic cliffside coastal drives and authentic Italian dining.
