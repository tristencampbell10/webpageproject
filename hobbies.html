What I Love Doing

A closer look at how each of these three hobbies keeps me driven, energized, and connected:

🏈 ATHLETICS
🏈
GROSSMONT FOOTBALL

High School Football

Playing football for Grossmont High School is my biggest athletic commitment. From daily team practices and weight training to studying schemes and competing under the lights, football teaches discipline, toughness, and brotherhood.

  • ⚡ Training: Speed intervals, strength, and agility drills
  • 🛡️ Teamwork: Executing assignments and trusting my squad
  • 🔥 Mindset: Giving 100% effort on every single play
#GrossmontHigh #FreshmanFootball #Gridiron

🎧
PLAYLISTS & TRACKS

Listening to Music

Music is playing almost anytime I'm not in class. High-energy hip-hop and rap get my adrenaline going in the locker room before games, while chill tracks help me stay locked in during homework and unwind after long practices.

  • 🎶 Genres: Hip-hop, workout rap, trap, and chill beats
  • 🔊 Routine: Curating pre-game playlists that build focus
  • 🔋 Impact: Setting the vibe and energizing daily workouts
#PreGameHype #WorkoutBeats #ChillPlaylists
🎮 GAMING
🎮
SQUAD & STRATEGY

Video Games

Gaming is the best way to jump on with teammates and friends after homework and practices are done. Whether running squad matches, sports games, or testing strategy, it combines fast reflexes with great team communication.

  • 🎯 Favorites: Competitive multiplayer, sports titles, and battle royales
  • 🎙️ Social: Squad comms, laughing, and teaming up with friends
  • 🧠 Skill: Quick decision-making and spatial awareness
#SquadGaming #Strategy #Multiplayer
THE ROUTINE

Keeping It All Balanced

Balancing school, football practice, music, and gaming comes down to managing time well. Getting homework and workouts done first allows me to listen to favorite tracks and hop into gaming sessions without stress.

🏈 On Field: Dedication, sweat, and team pride 🎧 In Headphones: Motivation and focus 🎮 On Screen: Teamwork and fun with friends
