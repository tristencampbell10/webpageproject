ACTIVITIES & PASSIONS

Hobbies & Personal Passions

What keeps me energized, curious, and motivated outside of school—from high school football on the gridiron to cinema, competitive gaming, web design, and outdoor adventures in San Diego.

Balancing Athletics, Curiosity & Life

Being a freshman student-athlete at Grossmont High School means balancing tough training on the football field with an active, curious lifestyle. My hobbies aren't just ways to pass the time—they fuel my discipline, creativity, and love for problem solving.

Whether dissecting cinematography in a gripping movie, coordinating tactical plays with teammates, building responsive web interfaces, or catching the ocean breeze along the San Diego coast, I believe in staying engaged and enjoying every moment.

Top Cinema & Series Recommendations

A few films and television shows that stand out for exceptional storytelling, cinematography, and compelling concepts:

Film • Sci-Fi / Thriller

Inception

Directed by Christopher Nolan (2010)

Unbelievable practical effects, multi-layered dream mechanics, and Hans Zimmer’s iconic brass score. A masterclass in original conceptual filmmaking.

Film • Sci-Fi / Adventure

Interstellar

Directed by Christopher Nolan (2014)

Breathtaking cosmic visuals paired with a deeply emotional core about time, family, and humanity's survival across uncharted wormholes.

Film • Action / Aerial

Top Gun: Maverick

Directed by Joseph Kosinski (2022)

Real cockpit aerial cinematography that delivers unmatched physical immersion and adrenaline. Perfect blend of teamwork, nostalgia, and precision.

Series • Mystery / Sci-Fi

Stranger Things

Created by The Duffer Brothers (Netflix)

Incredible 80s aesthetic, synthwave soundtrack, and endearing characters facing supernatural mysteries. Captures the spirit of adventure and loyalty.
