Roadmap & Vision

My 5-Year Journey Ahead

A structured five-year roadmap outlining where I plan to be, what I hope to study and build, and the realistic milestones guiding my growth from high school to a professional career in computer science.

Vision & Guiding Philosophy

As I look ahead toward the transition from high school to higher education, my primary ambition is to combine rigorous academic engineering foundations with hands-on software development. Over the next five years, I aim to immerse myself in undergraduate computer science research, open-source development, and industry internships.

Technology is at its best when it solves tangible human problems—whether that is streamlining student workflows, creating accessible tools for underserved communities, or designing resilient distributed web systems.

KEY MILESTONE

Specific Long-Term Goal

Goal: Complete a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science with Honors within four years of university enrollment, maintain a minimum 3.7 GPA, and author at least one peer-reviewed undergraduate research paper or production-grade open-source library used by more than 1,000 active developers.

🎯 Metric: B.S. Degree in Computer Science 📊 Standard: 3.7+ Cumulative GPA 🚀 Deliverable: Open-Source Tool or Paper

Five-Year Roadmap (2026 – 2031)

A phased breakdown of my academic, technical, and professional progression:

1
Year 1: 2026 – 2027

High School Graduation & University Transition

Conclude high school with high academic honors in AP Computer Science and AP Calculus. Transition into an accredited university computer science program, engage with campus engineering clubs, and establish study cohorts in discrete mathematics and foundational data structures.

2
Year 2: 2027 – 2028

Algorithms, Open Source & First Tech Internship

Deepen theoretical foundations in algorithms, database design, and systems programming. Contribute actively to open-source developer tooling and secure a summer software engineering internship focused on full-stack web platforms and cloud infrastructure.

3
Year 3: 2028 – 2029

Undergraduate Research & Systems Architecture

Partner with university faculty on applied research in distributed computing or artificial intelligence interfaces. Take on a leadership role as an engineering mentor for underclassmen and expand personal projects into scalable cloud applications.

4
Year 4: 2029 – 2030

Senior Capstone & Industry Co-op

Execute a comprehensive senior capstone project solving an enterprise or community challenge with modern web protocols. Complete a high-impact summer co-op at an innovative technology firm and prepare for post-graduate career opportunities.

5
Year 5: 2030 – 2031

B.S. Degree Completion & Full-Time Software Engineer

Graduate with a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science. Enter the technology industry as a full-time software engineer contributing to mission-critical distributed systems, while continuing to contribute to open-source developer communities and mentoring aspiring high school students.
