ROADMAP & DREAMS

College Football & The Road to LSU

My dedicated 5-year journey from Grossmont High School student-athlete to stepping onto the field as a college freshman playing football for Louisiana State University (LSU) in Baton Rouge.

Vision & The Tiger Dream

Ever since putting on pads, my ultimate collegiate dream has been clear: earn my spot at Louisiana State University (LSU) and represent the purple and gold under the roaring Saturday night lights of Tiger Stadium (Death Valley).

Reaching SEC Division 1 college football demands relentless work on and off the field. It takes grueling early morning weight sessions, film study, speed and agility drills, and maintaining strong academic discipline. As a freshman currently playing football at Grossmont High School, every snap and workout is a step toward making that collegiate dream a reality.

PRIMARY MILESTONE

LSU Freshman Football Student-Athlete

The Goal: Enroll as a freshman student-athlete at Louisiana State University (LSU) in Baton Rouge, earn a roster spot on the LSU Tigers football team, compete in the SEC, and pursue a college degree with strong academic standing.

🐯 Target School: Louisiana State University (LSU) 🏈 Athletic Level: NCAA Division 1 SEC Football 🎓 Academics: 3.5+ GPA & College Degree Track 📍 Destination: Baton Rouge & Death Valley

Five-Year Football & Academic Roadmap (2026 – 2031)

The strategic progression from high school freshman to collegiate freshman on the LSU gridiron:

1
Year 1: 2026 – 2027

High School Freshmen Season & Athletic Foundation

Compete on the Grossmont High School freshmen squad, master the playbook, and establish physical discipline. Build explosive speed, agility, and core strength in the weight room while holding a strong academic GPA to build an unbreakable college eligibility baseline.

2
Year 2: 2027 – 2028

Varsity Football Ascension & Regional Showcases

Transition to Grossmont's Varsity roster, earning consistent playing time through relentless motor and technique. Attend summer collegiate prospect camps, 7-on-7 tournaments, and track & field sprint events to increase top-end speed and football IQ.

3
Year 3: 2028 – 2029

Junior Season Breakout & College Recruiting Circuit

Deliver an All-League junior campaign with high-impact film highlights. Connect with college scouts and recruiting coordinators, take unofficial college visits to Baton Rouge to experience LSU game days in Tiger Stadium, and excel on college entrance exams.

4
Year 4: 2029 – 2030

Senior Leadership, Championship Push & LSU Commitment

Lead Grossmont High as a senior captain into CIF playoff contention. Finalize college enrollment decisions on National Signing Day with Louisiana State University (LSU), graduate with honors, and begin collegiate off-season conditioning programs.

5
Year 5: 2030 – 2031

LSU Freshman Year & Death Valley Football

Arrive in Baton Rouge as an LSU freshman student-athlete! Join summer workouts, grind through SEC fall training camp, suit up in purple and gold at Tiger Stadium, and balance college coursework while living out the dream of playing Division 1 college football.
